What a receipt buys you

The receipt binds the content hashes of the artifacts in this delivery, not their URLs. That distinction matters: a site can move while its receipt stays valid, and a receipt that only recorded a URL could silently point at something else. Both receipts are retained here, including the superseded one, because a revision history that hides its own earlier state is not auditable.

  • Revision 0 (superseded): first pass, published on a handle subdomain. Kept at /receipts/sha256-30cda465….json.
  • Revision 1 (current): re-hosted on this registered project domain, artifacts re-bound, receipt re-sealed.

Why the revision: the first pass lived on a <handle>.github.io address, which a reader has no reason to treat as a durable home. The documentation itself was already sound; the surface was not. See delivery practice for what that failure mode looks like in general.
